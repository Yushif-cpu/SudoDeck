          Web Traffic in OSINT & Threat Hunting
        

        

          Security analysts and threat hunters leverage traffic metrics to evaluate domain legitimacy, audience exposure, and footprint credibility. Sudden visitor spikes or unindexed newly registered domains often correlate with active phishing lures, typosquatting assets, or malicious C2 infrastructure.
        

      


      

        

          
          Supabase Hybrid Caching Engine
        

        

          Every query first checks the high-performance Supabase PostgreSQL store. Cached records resolve in sub-milliseconds to optimize Apify quotas. When a domain is uncached or force-refreshed, Apify Similarweb Scraper extracts real-time telemetry and stores it for subsequent queries.
